How to Play

Use arrow keys [↑→↓←] or W-A-S-D keys to move Mario, to jump higher hold the button.

Use Shift/CTRL to Fire/Sprint. Use P to pause and M to mute.

Gameplay

In this game you play as Mario. Navigate through the Mushroom Kingdom, avoiding enemies and collecting coins. Reach the flag pole at the end of the level to complete it!

Move Left Left Arrow or A
Move Right Right Arrow or D
Jump Up Arrow, W, or Space
Sprint Shift or Control
Pause P
Mute M
